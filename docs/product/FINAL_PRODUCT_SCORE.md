## Verdict

**Enterprise Ready with Future Enhancements** for on-boarded (managed) sales.
**Design-Partner Ready** for early SaaS customers.
**Not yet ready** for open, fully self-serve public SaaS until Waves 1–3 land (tenant lifecycle, subscription billing, branding + setup wizard).

All recommendations in this review are non-behavioral. No runtime, authorization, RLS, SECURITY DEFINER, Edge Function, schema, bundle, or permission changes are proposed.
